# MediAssist Pro — User Guide (First Time Users)

Welcome to **MediAssist Pro**, your AI-powered clinical assistant. This guide will help you navigate your first intake session.

## 1. Getting Started
1. **Launch the App:** Double-click `MediAssistPro.exe`.
2. **Login:** Enter your assigned username. For your first time, you can select the **Admin** role to configure the system.

## 2. Running your first Patient Intake
1. Navigate to the **Patient Management** tab in the sidebar.
2. **Patient ID:** Enter a Case Number (or leave blank to auto-generate one).
3. **Fetch Details:** If the patient has visited before, click "Fetch" to load their history.
4. **Visit Details:** Select the **Visit Type** (e.g., General Checkup) and the **Clinical Domain** (e.g., Cardiology).
5. **Start Intake:** Click the teal "Start AI Clinical Intake" button.

## 3. The 4-Round Questionnaire
- **Round 1:** The AI will ask about symptoms and urgent red flags.
- **Rounds 2-4:** The questions will adapt based on your previous answers. 
- **Citations:** Look for source names (e.g., "According to WHO...") to see where the AI got its information.
- **Navigation:** Answer all mandatory questions to proceed to the next round.

## 4. Vital Signs & Report
1. After Round 4, enter the patient's vitals (BP, Heart Rate, etc.).
2. Click **Generate Report**. 
3. The **Physician Brief** will appear, showing Clinical Flags (Red/Amber) and Differential Diagnoses.

## 5. Physician Sign-off
- If you are a doctor, go to the **Physician Dashboard**.
- Select a patient, review the report, add your clinical notes, and click **Sign & Lock**.
