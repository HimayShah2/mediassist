
    # Questionnaire Structure Report
    
    Round 1: Triage & Initial Screening
    - Inputs: Text (Chief Complaint), MCQ (Duration, Severity).
    - Goal: Determine urgency and categorize the primary issue.
    
    Round 2: Symptom Deep-Dive (OPQRST)
    - Inputs: Scale (Pain), MCQ (Onset, Provocation, Quality, Radiation, Site, Timing).
    - Goal: Clinical characterization using RAG-sourced medical guidelines.
    
    Round 3: Systems Review & Risk Factors
    - Inputs: MCQ (Allergies, Current Meds), Checkbox (Past Medical History).
    - Goal: Identify comorbidities and contraindications.
    
    Round 4: Differential Diagnosis Refinement
    - Inputs: Body Map (Specific localized pain), Date/Duration (Course of illness).
    - Goal: Final evidence gathering to distinguish between top 3 AI differentials.
